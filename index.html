✦ The Story Behind the Stones ✦

Shohei — the soul who remembered

Shohei has always been sensitive — deeply, achingly so. As an HSP, introvert, and someone living with anxiety, the world often felt too loud, too heavy, too much. For years he struggled to reconcile his nature with the world's expectations of who he should be.

"The stones didn't change me. They reminded me of who I already was."

It was crystals that helped him find his way back. Stone by stone, he learned to honour his sensitivity as a gift, not a wound. That journey led him inward — and outward to the football pitch, where in 2025 he became Japan National Champion of Freestyle Football.

Along the way, he laced up his boots and disappeared into the wild Celtic landscape — backpacking alone through ancient highlands, misty cliffs and valleys carved by time. In that silence, surrounded by rock and sky, the earth spoke. It confirmed what the stones had always whispered: that solitude is not loneliness, and sensitivity is not weakness.

His soul carries the memory of Lemuria, the ancient celestial civilization — and so does every stone in this shop. Silent Hope Orchestra was born to share that remembrance with you.
